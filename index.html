The difference

Review is the product, not an afterthought

Most "AI notes" apps write straight into your file. canonote gives the agent a pen it cannot use on its own.

  1. 01

    Your agent reads and proposes

    It works against the note you are reading and sends a suggestion. It has no way to rewrite the note itself.

  2. 02

    You see what would change

    The suggestion arrives with the original text and the proposed replacement, next to the note — a diff you can read, not a version already on disk.

  3. 03

    You accept, or you reject

    Accepting applies the change and records it in the activity feed. Rejecting leaves the note exactly as it was.

Honest scope: the notebook itself needs no backend at all. Reviewing agent suggestions uses the optional hosted layer (Supabase). Without it you still get the full editor, search, export and version history.

What it is
